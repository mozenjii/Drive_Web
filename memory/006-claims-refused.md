# Claims found and deliberately not reproduced

`sites/VERIFY.md` forbids publishing an unverified pass rate, rating, review count or student
count. Every one of these is on the prospect's own site. None of it is on any preview.

Recorded here because the temptation to "just use what they already say" will come back, and
because several of these are worth raising **with** the client as a reason to work with us.

| Client | On their own site | Why it is not on the preview |
|---|---|---|
| Bill's | "95% first time pass rate"; "28 Teachers \| 20k+ Licenses Issued \| 5+ Working Years" | Unverifiable, and the five years contradicts a 2014 founding date on the same page |
| NorCal | "high pass rate (98%!)", stated three times | Unverifiable |
| Newport | "99% PASSING RATE"; "224000+ SATISFIED CUSTOMERS" | 224,000 over 29 years is 21 customers every single day |
| Academic | "99% of DMV drive test passing rate"; "the topest driving school in California" | Unverifiable, and a superlative claim |
| MAS | "95% First-Time Pass Rate" | Unverifiable |
| H and G | "1.5K+ 5-Star Ratings" | Google shows 162 reviews |
| A1 | "30,000+ graduates"; "550+ five-star reviews" | Google shows 93 reviews |
| Teen Driving Academy | "over 50,000 happy clients" | Unverifiable |
| Dollar | "Highest Passing rate" | Superlative, unverifiable |
| Driving School For You | third-party directory rankings | Not theirs to claim |

**One claim was reproduced.** MAS's "2,500+ 5-star Google reviews" — Google shows 2,833 at 5.0,
so it checks out.

## Also refused

- **A1's competitor copy.** Their Bakersfield page names two competitors, ranks them second and
  third, and quotes one of them. That is live defamation and unfair-competition exposure. Not
  reproduced, and flagged to raise with them — it is a legal problem, not a design problem.
- **San Diego Bay's AI-generated imagery.** Only their two real photographs were used.
- **Synthetic faces** for any named real person.
- **Invented prices.** Five schools publish none. Their pricing sections say so.

## Licence numbers

Two schools publish **no DMV licence number anywhere**: Sanctified and AllGood. Both previews
omit the field, and both `internalNotes` flag it as the first thing to ask for.
