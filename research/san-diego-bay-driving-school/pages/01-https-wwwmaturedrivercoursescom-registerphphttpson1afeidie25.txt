Why take our Mature Driver Improvement Online Course?
Mature Driver Courses has been in the Driver and Traffic Safety industry for 35 years. Our Calif. DMV Approved refresher course for drivers age 55 and older helps drivers remain safe on today's roads. Our course is very simple to use and designed to help maintain your safe driving record. Our course is very simple to use and designed to help you:
1 Tune up your driving skills and update your knowledge of the rules of the road.
2 Learn about normal age-related physical changes, and how to adjust your driving to allow for these changes.
3 Reduce your traffic violations, accidents, and chances for injuries.
4 With the use of videos, animation and easy to read short chapters, you will be able to retain the information being presented to you!
