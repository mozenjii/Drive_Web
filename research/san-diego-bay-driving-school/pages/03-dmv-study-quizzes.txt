At San Diego Bay Driving School, our DMV Written Test Preparation Program is designed to help students study efficiently, confidently, and at their own pace by providing full access to a comprehensive DMV test bank with hundreds of real-style questions and study guides based on the California DMV handbook.
The program is completely self-guided and self-paced, allowing students to complete practice tests in organized sections that are easy to understand and convenient to review, with unlimited logins, unlimited retakes, and the ability to repeat tests as many times as needed.
This system supports over 100 languages, making it accessible to a wide range of learners, whether they are preparing for their first permit test or reviewing after a failed attempt. Everything is included in one simple program for a low, one-time price of $21.95, giving students all the tools they need to prepare and succeed on the DMV written exam.
