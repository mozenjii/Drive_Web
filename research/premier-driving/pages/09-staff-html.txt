NON LICENSED CANDIDATES:

California DMV-licensed Driving Instructor requires the applicant to be at least 21 years of age with no felonies or moral convictions, he or she must provide proof of high school graduation, and pass a physical examination . in order to obtain a DMV Occupational driving instructor License, candidate must first be sponsored by a licensed California driving school (a school that intends to employ you as a Driving Instructor) and APPLICANT must submit A FINGERPRINT SCAN for BACKGROUND check, After receiving clearance candidate can begin training program. entire training program involves 60 hours, including 30 hours of classroom time and 30 hours in the vehicle. After the 60 hours of instructor training has been completed, CALIFORNIA DMV-LICENSED DRIVING INSTRUCTOR Occupational License may be obtained after the applicant goes to the DMV and provides a fingerprint background clearance, submits an application for a background check, pays a $35 fee, and passes a written test.
