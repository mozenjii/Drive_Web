Driving training packages

Short explanation of above in car (behind the wheel) driving training as below.
​
10 Hours of Behind the Wheel Lesson:
Ten hours of behind the wheel driving lesson is a comprehensive package. This program will cover all the driving techniques on the road, intense freeway training (entering, exiting, merging, passing, being passed, line changes), and other advanced tactics to make the student a safe defensive driver. The program consist of 5 classes, each class is 2 hours and teaches one class/day.
​6 Hours of Behind the Wheel Lesson:
This is a in-depth program which contains of 6 hours of behind-the-wheel driving education by the expert driving school instructors. This package of training is divided into three lessons each lesson will take two hours. Students will learn basic driving skills, proper driving techniques, road side traffic signs, safe driving and will enable to drive a motor vehicle safely. At the end of the courses student will be prepared for the DMV test too.
​2 Hours refresher in car training:
This package is 2 Hours for those who want to have a refresher in car driving training, this is a professional driving lesson with a qualified and expert instructor.
20 Hours behind the wheel lesson:
This package is the extended hours, it covers everything of all above packages plus more hours of practice.
​DMV Drive Test + 1 hour of Drive training
Respected clients and our students can use our Dual-Control-Car for your DMV Drive Test (road test). This package is for those students who want to have a fresh driving practice on the day of DMV test. It is a 3-hour program that includes 1 hours of professional training + 2 hours of Drive Test that empower the student to learn and clench driving test techniques and skills.
We provide behind the wheel driving training for minors, Adults and Seniors.
We Issue Certificates for Minors to certify the 6 hours of behind the wheel trainings.
We offer free pick-up and Drop off from your home, work or school (at your location).
We offer new cars with dual gas and brake system for the safety of our students.
