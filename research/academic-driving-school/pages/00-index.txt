Driving training packages

Behind-the-Wheel Driving Training Packages
10 Hours of Behind-the-Wheel Lesson:
This comprehensive program consists of ten hours of behind-the-wheel instruction, divided into five classes of two hours each. Students will master essential driving techniques, freeway navigation (including merging and lane changes), and advanced defensive driving strategies, ensuring they become safe, confident drivers.
6 Hours of Behind-the-Wheel Lesson:
This in-depth training package includes six hours of behind-the-wheel education, split into three two-hour lessons. Students will develop basic driving skills, learn proper techniques, recognize traffic signs, and practice safe driving. By the end of this course, they will be well-prepared for the DMV test.
2-Hour Refresher In-Car Training:
Designed for those seeking to brush up on their driving skills, this two-hour refresher lesson is led by qualified instructors. It provides a focused review of essential driving techniques and safety practices.
20 Hours Behind-the-Wheel Lesson:
This extended package offers comprehensive training, combining elements from all previous packages with additional practice hours. It is ideal for those seeking thorough preparation and confidence behind the wheel.
DMV Drive Test + 1 Hour of Driving Training:
In this package, students can utilize our dual-control car for their DMV drive test. This three-hour program includes one hour of professional training followed by two hours for the DMV test, allowing students to refine their skills and techniques right before the test.
Additional Offerings:
We provide behind-the-wheel training for minors, adults, and seniors.
Certificates for minors are issued upon completion of the six-hour training.
Complimentary pick-up and drop-off services from home, work, or school.
Our training vehicles are equipped with dual gas and brake systems for enhanced safety.
