Online Driver’s Ed
Ventura, Santa Barbara and Thousand Oaks Areas
If you’re under 18 and you don’t already have a permit, this is the place to start.
Easy Street’s Online Drivers Education Course is interesting, engaging and specifically designed to give you a complete understanding of safe driving laws in California. It satisfies the CA DMV requirement for students under 18yrs of age who want to apply for a California Driver’s Permit.
Quizzes are presented throughout to keep the you engaged and aid in retention of the material. Work at your own pace and gather all of the information needed to pass your DMV Knowledge Test.
We also strongly recommend reading the
California Driver Handbook
At the conclusion of the course,
we will mail (USPS) the required Certificate of Completion
to your home for free!
If you’re under 18 and you don’t already have a permit, select your location below and choose a package that supplies you with all the training the DMV requires to get your permit AND your license!
