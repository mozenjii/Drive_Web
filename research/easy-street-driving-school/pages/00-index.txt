Trustworthy Instructors

The idea of putting your teen in a car with a total stranger for 2 hours can be a little unsettling. In addition to the Live Scan background check, each potential driving instructor candidate is critically screened. We make sure each Instructor is friendly, caring and enjoys teaching teenagers. Most of all, we will not hire an instructor unless we would feel safe putting them in the same car with our own children.
We also extensively and continually train our instructors. Each Easy Street vehicle is equipped with a camera that records video and audio inside outside the car for training and safety purposes. We’re working hard so you can trust each and every one of our instructors.

Customized Lessons

Learn at your own pace in a way that suites your needs in an informative, relaxed and fun way. We have a variety of lesson packages available to help you drive as safe and confidently as possible.
We offer door-to-door pick up and drop off at your home, school, or work place.
After each lesson, your instructor will review your progress and tailor a practice plan specifically designed for you.

Quality Customer Service

With our easy to use website you can get answers to many of your questions, and register for online driver’s education whenever and wherever you want!
However, if you need behind-the-wheel lessons, it’s best that you call our friendly and knowledgeable office staff, so they can steer you in the right direction.
If you would like to meet us in person, please feel free to visit our office and we would love to help you any way we can.
Whether it’s your lesson, your account or your questions… you come first!
