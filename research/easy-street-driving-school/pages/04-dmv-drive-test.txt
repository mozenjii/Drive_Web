DMV Drive Test Service in Ventura and Santa Barbara

Use an Easy Street Vehicle to take the
DMV Drive Test
.
(Ventura, Goleta and Thousand Oaks DMVs Only)
Here’s how it works:
1. Get your California Learner’s Permit.
2. Take at least one lesson with Easy Street.
3. Obtain approval from an Easy Street instructor that you are ready for the DMV Drive Test.

4. Call the Easy Street office:
The Easy Street Office Staff will assist you to schedule your Drive Test at the Ventura or Goleta DMV.
The Easy Street Office Staff will provide you with the time to meet the instructor at the DMV.
5. Meet the Instructor at the DMV 30 minutes before the scheduled test time. (Be sure to make arrangements for a ride home after the test.)
Your Instructor will bring an Easy Street vehicle for you to use for your test.
Your instructor will escort you through the check-in process and wait in the instruction vehicle with you until your test begins.
Your instructor will wait for you at the DMV while you take the drive test.
You pass the test.
You leave the DMV with your very own California Drivers License and the pride of a job well done.

6. You drive safely and confidently for the rest of your life!
Contact our Friendly Office Staff to Answer any Questions or to Purchase any Lessons.

Please call (805) 685-2436 to schedule your Drive Test
