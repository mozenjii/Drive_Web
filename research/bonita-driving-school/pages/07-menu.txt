How to get your license

1) Online Drivers Ed

Once you complete the course you will receive a pink certificate on the mail directly to your home address

2) Permit test

Take the permit test at the dmv. Make sure to bring SS card, proof of residency, birth certificate, payment and pink certificate

3) Behind the wheel training

Once you pass the permit test, email us to schedule an appointment, validate your permit, take your 6 hours behind the wheel and receive your yellow certificate (Certificate of completion)

4) Take your drivers test

Make sure you finished your 6 hrs (3 lessons) with us and practice for 50 hours with parents before you take the drivers test
