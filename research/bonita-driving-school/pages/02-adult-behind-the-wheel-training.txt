Adult (18+) Behind The Wheel Lessons

*Student must have a learners permit
We start and end the lesson at your residence, college, or workplace
Throughout the lessons we will be driving around San Diego (Coronado, Downtown, Mission Valley, sunset cliffs, Chula Vista, etc...)
Our drivers training cars are modified with the gas and brake on the passenger side
We charge 120/hour. Each lesson is 2 hours. Therefore 240 per lesson
No commitment. Pay per lesson
There is a $40 cancelation fee if the lesson is canceled within 24 hours
