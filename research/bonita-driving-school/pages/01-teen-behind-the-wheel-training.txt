TEEN: Behind the wheel training

*Student must have a learners permit
Lessons start and end at student’s residence or school. Our drivers training cars are dual control; modified with the gas and brake on both sides
Throughout the lessons we will be driving around San Diego (Coronado, Downtown, Mission Valley, Sunset Cliffs, Ocean Beach etc...)
On the 3rd lesson we will issue the certificate of completion for students under 18 years of age
We charge 120/hour. Each lesson is 2 hours therefore 240 per session.
You do not need to pay for the full 6 hours upfront. You pay per lesson.
There is a $40 cancelation fee if a lesson is canceled within 24 hours of appointment.
