WHO WE ARE

Licensed and certified instructors by the DMV
Family owned and operate business since 1980s
At Bonita Driving School it is our mission to take our students by the hand, from the first time you sit behind the wheel till you get your license, and provide the best care, safety and quality instruction available.

We’re available!
We are local, knowledgeable and friendly, and our primary goal is YOUR SAFETY. We treat our students as though we were teaching our own children and family members to drive. We genuinely care about our students’ well-being, and we want them to have the most and best information and instruction available. We care deeply about our students, and the mission that drives Safety 1st: to provide the best quality Driver’s Education to our students in a caring and safe environment.
