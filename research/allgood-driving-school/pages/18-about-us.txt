20+ Years Helping Good Drivers!
AllGood Driving School Inc. was founded in 1996 (when this picture was taken) by Mike Thomas and Tessa Zertuche. It's hard to believe that it has been more than 20 years helping new and experienced drivers to become Good Drivers. We're ready for 20 more!
WHAT WE DO:
We offer Online Teenage Driver Education throughout all of California. We offer Behind The Wheel Driver Training in Sacramento County, San Joaquin County, Sonoma County, Marin County, Calaveras County, Tuolumne County, and some parts of Yolo and Placer Counties. We are experts in helping new drivers obtain a license for the first time, as well as helping experienced drivers maintain their licenses and driving records.
OUR MISSION:
To make All Drivers, Good Drivers, through quality education and communication. We hope to see new drivers obtain their driver's licenses with ease, knowledge, and the confidence that it takes to be a safe driver on today's roadways. We also hope to see existing drivers participate in the process of promoting safety, and learning new things, through communication with both new and experienced drivers.
OUR INSTRUCTORS:
Our Driving Instructors are friendly, patient, and knowledgeable. Each one is highly trained in industry standard techniques, as well as our own proprietary methods of driving instruction. They will help to create a calm, comfortable and stress free environment to maximize your learning potential, and help make the experience more enjoyable for you. Each instructor has undergone and passed a thorough background check performed by the Department of Justice. They will arrive to pick you up for your lesson in an easily identifiable car with our name and logo on the side. Each one will also have a picture I.D. issued by our company in addition to their regular driver license and DMV issued instructor license.
