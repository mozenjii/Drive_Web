Description
Our internet study course is reviewed and approved by the state of California DMV, and fulfills the Driver Education requirements for students to receive their learner’s permit as young as the age 15 1/2. Valid for all California residents. This course has helped more than 30,000 California Teens receive their driver's license.
How Does It Work?
Simply click the enroll / start now button above, enter your enrollment information, and pay for the course. You will be able to begin your course immediately. Our course is optimized to work on most devices and browsers. You can even use your cell phone, although it may be easier on a larger device. Upon completion of all lessons and quizzes, your DMV certificate will be mailed via USPS since the DMV does not accept digital copies. Certificate processing may take up to 2 business days, and mail time may take up to 3 business days.
Additional Details
Busy on your weekends? Don’t want to wake up extra early or stay after school to take another class? Already 15 ½ years old and want to start driving? Internet Driver Education may be the answer for you! You simply create a username and password to access the course. You work on the course at your own pace and according to your own schedule. A midterm exam and a final exam are taken online and are graded immediately. A score of at least 70% is required to pass the course. If you fail a test, you may re-take the test as many times as necessary at no extra charge. Upon successful completion of the course, we mail a DMV completion certificate to you right away so you can get your permit. It’s that easy! And, what’s best about it, you can work on it 7 hours a day or 1 hour a day, at 2:00 in the morning or at 3:00 in the afternoon. It’s up to you!

Our internet course is approved by the Department of Motor Vehicles in the state of California and fulfills the driver education requirements for students to receive their learner’s permits as young as age 15 ½ .

Don’t be fooled by other courses! Many companies that offer driver education courses simply provide a course that is mostly a reproduced version of the driver handbook – a publication that can be picked up for free from the DMV or downloaded from the DMV website. A quality driver’s education is critical, so don’t take an inferior course.

Our course was created and designed by professional driving instructors with 20 years of experience helping thousands of teens get their licenses. We utilize almost 200 color graphics and photos to enhance the student’s learning experience. Many of these graphics are interactive, allowing the students to make their own decisions about proper driving techniques.

Since we feel that parents are a critical factor in a student’s success as a safe driver, our course also offers numerous parental involvement activities. These activities help give parents the tools and confidence necessary to play a powerful role in the driver education process.

Online Course Tech Support
To login to your course, please use the orange Login to My Course button at the top of this page. If you have forgotten your username and password, then select that same button, and then select the support/forgotten your password buttons to retrieve your password. If you need to enroll still, use the Enroll/Start now button at the top of this page.

Our online course must be able to communicate with your computer in order for you to login, and for it to save your forward progress through the course. It does this by placing a cookie on your computer. Most cookies (including ours) are totally safe. They are used by most websites today in order to keep track of your important interactions with the website. For example, Amazon.com uses a cookie to keep track of items that you may place in a shopping cart. These cookies will not harm your computer, and they are not used to obtain personal information from you. They are simply used to make your internet experience better.

In any case, if you are having login problems, or if your progress through our course is not being recorded, it is most likely due to the fact that you are either blocking cookies from our website, or that a previously placed cookie has become expired or corrupted. The first steps to fix this are as follows, and pertain to most internet browsers.

Close all open browsers.

Then re-open your browser and go to the settings. Delete all temporary internet files, and delete all cookies.

Then make sure that you are not blocking cookies from allgooddrivers.info

Then add allgooddrivers.info to the list of sites where cookies are accepted.

Then return to our site, and attempt to login and take the course again.

If you still have problems, and you have security/antivirus/anti spyware software, then you may need to adjust it's settings as well. It is rare that these programs effect our course, but some of them have very aggressive settings that may be adjusted to block everything from anyone(including important cookies). To test this you may want to temporarily turn it off, then access the course and see if it works. If it does, then you know you need to adjust the settings within your security software.

If you do not have security software and are still having problems, then you likely have a virus or spyware that is interfering with our cookie. You may need to install some security software to fix the issues.
If after all this you are still having problems, then you will need to contact us using the "contact us" link on the left.
