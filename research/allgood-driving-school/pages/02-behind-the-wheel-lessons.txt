Requirements for Teens under 18:
Teens are required by DMV to complete 6 hours of Behind the Wheel Driving Lessons with a professional licensed driving school prior to taking a DMV drive test for a driver license. We conduct our lessons in two hour increments, for a total of 3 lessons required before a teen may return to DMV for a drive test. You will need your permit before you can do any lessons. You may schedule your appointments at any time you like, but we recommend taking your first lesson right after you get your permit, your second lesson a couple months later, and your third lesson a couple weeks before taking your drive test.
YOU MAY NOT TAKE MORE THAN ONE LESSON PER DAY, PER DMV REGULATION.

Payment, Cancellation, and Refund Policy

PAYMENTS:

All payments are due at the time of booking.

Payments can be made via, Visa, MC, American Express and Discover, both by credit card, or debit card.

CANCELLATIONS:

MORE THAN 24 HOURS NOTICE: If you wish to cancel or re-schedule an appoint with more than 24 hours notice, you may do so by using the link contained in the original confirmation email that you received. Cancellations or schedule changes made more than 24 hours in advance of an appointment can be made with no charge. A refund will be issued to the original payment source.

2- 24 HOURS NOTICE: Cancellations or re-schedules made with 2- 24 hours notice, MUST be done by texting 916-866-8051 as fees will apply. Cancellations or schedule changes made with 2- 24 hours notice will result in a $20 rescheduling fee. A refund will be applied to the original payment source, minus the $20 fee.

LESS THAN TWO HOURS NOTICE OR STUDENT NO SHOW: Cancellations or re-schedules made with less than 2 hours notice, MUST be done by texting 916-866-8051 as fees will apply. Cancellations or No-Shows which are within two hours of the scheduled appointment, or are the result of the instructor showing up for the lesson, and the student either cancels at the time of the lesson, or is not present, or does not have proof of a valid permit, will result in a $60 no-show fee. A refund will be applied to the original payment source, minus the $60 fee.

REFUNDS:

Upon request, refunds will be applied to the original source, minus any applicable cancellation fees.

ADDITIONALLY:

In the case of inclement weather, appointments are only cancelled in the event of ice or snow sticking to the roadways, or at the discretion of the instructor to ensure safety.

Students are responsible for the appointments they schedule through our website, as tracked by our software, regardless of if they received text or emailed confirmations or reminders. You are encouraged to text us if you feel you are not receiving notifications from us and we will try to determine why.

Adults 18 and over are not required by DMV to take any behind the wheel driving lessons. Use of Our Car for DMV Drive Test:
Before obtaining a license, every student must take a driving test at the DMV. Students may use any vehicle for this test, and we feel that students should choose a vehicle that they are comfortable with. The vehicle used must meet the DMV requirements to be used for a drive test. Students may choose to pay an extra fee to use our vehicle for this test. If a student wants to use our vehicle for the test, they schedule through our website, and we will schedule the DMV test time for them. The following options are available for using our vehicle for the test. If you are under 18, these options are only available to those who have already completed the required 6 hours of behind the wheel training. For those 18 or older, these option are only available to those who have already completed at least one 2 hour lesson with us. The use of our vehicle for a drive test is at our discretion. If we feel a student is not at an ability level that we determine to be sufficient, then they will not be allowed to use our car for the test.

The benefits to using our vehicles are:
*We pick the student up early for the test so that they can warm up and review for the exam.
*Our vehicles are currently registered and insured, which is required of the testing vehicle, and they meet all the safety requirements necessary for a vehicle to qualify for use in a drive test.
*Students may feel more comfortable in our vehicles since they are automatic transmissions, small, and easy to drive.
*DMV examiners may feel more comfortable while testing you because there is a brake and rear view mirrors for them, and they will know that you have done some training with us.

You must have completed at least one lesson with us, and be of an acceptable skill level (as determined by our instructors), to be eligible to use our car for a behind the wheel drive test at the DMV. Students under the age of 18 must have completed all 6 hours of behind the wheel training prior to using this service.
