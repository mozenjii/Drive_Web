About Us

Easy & Affordable is a DMV licensed school and was founded on the belief that every student has the ability to learn.

Over the past 23 years, our staff has helped more than 20,000 students get their license and improve their driving skills, comprehension rules and regulations, and defensive driving skills. As a result, these students have earned better test scores and drive more safely, and grown in self-esteem and confidence. We love celebrating those victories with our students.

In a recent survey, 98% of the parents who participated would recommend the Easy & Affordable to a friend.
