Driving Lessons

Built for new, nervous, and test-ready drivers.

Start with quiet neighborhoods and core vehicle control, then progress to major roads, freeway readiness, defensive driving, and DMV road-test preparation.

Teen Driving Lessons

For students with permits who are required professional behind-the-wheel instruction by the DMV. Develop confidence and safe driving habits before the drive test.

Adult Driving Lessons

For first time adult learners, returning drivers, and anyone who wants a focus refresher behind-the-wheel to prepare for a DMV road test.

Road Test Preparation

Your instructor will review key road-test skills, identify areas of weakness and give you a clear feedback for final practice.

Costa Mesa Area Training

Practice in real driving conditions around Costa Mesa.

Costa Mesa offers an ideal driving environment with residential neighborhoods, busy intersections, and nearby freeway access that help students develop real-world driving confidence. As students progress, lessons include city traffic navigation, freeway driving, and defensive driving techniques throughout the Costa Mesa area and surrounding Orange County roads.

Smooth acceleration, braking, and steering.

Lane changes, turns, and intersection awareness.

Parking, reversing, and curb judgment.

Defensive driving and hazard scanning.

Confidence for DMV road-test situations.

Safety First

Trusted Instructors

Patient, professional instructors who focus on clear coaching, confidence building, and safe decision-making.

Training Vehicle

Post-Lesson Feedback

License Guide for Minors

Not sure where to start? Follow these steps.

1

Pass Drivers Ed

Complete a state-approved Drivers Ed course before taking the DMV written test.

2

Practice Written Test

Build confidence with practice written tests and examine road signs and traffic laws.

3

Get Your Permit

Schedule your DMV appointment, pass the written test, and receive your permit.

4

Take Driving Lessons

Book your behind-the-wheel lessons with patient instructors who will help you master the road.

5

Pass the Road Test

Prepare for your road test, sharpen your weak spots and arrive ready for your DMV road test.

About
With over 28 years of trusted service, Newport Driving School has built a reputation as Southern California’s leading provider of personalized driving instruction. Our deep experience allows us to meet the diverse needs of drivers of all ages, abilities, and skill levels—whether you're a first-time learner or looking to refresh your skills. Better Drivers, Safer Roads isn’t just our slogan—it’s the mission that drives everything we do.

At the heart of our program is a team of professional, DMV-certified instructors dedicated to your success behind the wheel. They begin by carefully evaluating your unique background, experience, and skill level—taking the time to truly understand where you’re starting from. Only then do they design a personalized lesson plan tailored specifically to your needs. The result? A custom driving course that not only prepares you to earn your license, but also builds your confidence and control on the road. We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all instruction—because every driver is different, and every lesson should be too.
