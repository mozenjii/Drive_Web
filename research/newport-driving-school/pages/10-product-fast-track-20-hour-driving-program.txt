Fast Track 20-Hour Driving Program
$
2,170.00

Our Fast Track 20-Hour Program is an intensive driving course designed for students who want to learn quickly and prepare confidently for their road test at the California Department of Motor Vehicles.

This program includes 20 hours of behind-the-wheel driving instruction with a professional instructor. Lessons are scheduled in an accelerated format so you can build skills faster than traditional weekly lessons and be ready for your DMV road test sooner.

What’s Included

18 Hours of Behind-the-Wheel Training with a certified instructor

2-Hour Road Test Preparation Lesson focused on DMV test routes and common mistakes

DMV Road Test Appointment Scheduling – we handle the booking for you

Use of the School Car for the Road Test at the California Department of Motor Vehicles

Who This Program Is For?

✔ New drivers who want structured, intensive training

✔ Students who want to prepare quickly for the DMV road test

✔ Drivers who want extra practice to build confidence and safety skills

Train faster. Gain confidence. Pass your DMV road test.

About
With over 28 years of trusted service, Newport Driving School has built a reputation as Southern California’s leading provider of personalized driving instruction. Our deep experience allows us to meet the diverse needs of drivers of all ages, abilities, and skill levels—whether you're a first-time learner or looking to refresh your skills. Better Drivers, Safer Roads isn’t just our slogan—it’s the mission that drives everything we do.

At the heart of our program is a team of professional, DMV-certified instructors dedicated to your success behind the wheel. They begin by carefully evaluating your unique background, experience, and skill level—taking the time to truly understand where you’re starting from. Only then do they design a personalized lesson plan tailored specifically to your needs. The result? A custom driving course that not only prepares you to earn your license, but also builds your confidence and control on the road. We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all instruction—because every driver is different, and every lesson should be too.
