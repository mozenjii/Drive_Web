Description

Description

The Test Day Drive is a package that lasts for the whole DMV Test duration (no training).

School provides insured and dual-controlled (instructor has an additional brake pedal on the passenger side) cars for student testing at no additional cost.

Student pick-up and drop-off is available at no additional cost (within 10 miles radius).

Choose your preferred licensed instructor to test with (after checkout).

Book, reschedule and cancel your appointments online.

A typical Test Day Drive appointment may include:

Student drive from pick-up location to nearby DMV office.

Test preparation and overview on the way to the DMV office.

Instructor help student check-in and wait in-line.

Student drive school insured car for their DMV test.

Student review testing results with licensed instructor.

NOTE: Student shall book a drive test appointment with the DMV
before
scheduling a Test Day Drive appointment with our licensed instructor.
