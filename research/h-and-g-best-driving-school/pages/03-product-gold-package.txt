Description

Description

The Gold Package is a 5 training session offer that is 2 hours long each (10 hours total).

School provides insured and dual-controlled (instructor has an additional brake pedal on the passenger side) cars for student training and testing at no additional cost.

Student pick-up and drop-off is available at no additional cost (within 10 miles radius).

Choose your preferred licensed instructor to train with (after checkout).

Book, reschedule and cancel your appointments online.

Proposed Package Session Trainings

As needed by students, the
1st
drive training session may include:

Pre-check list.

Driving preparation.

Understanding steering control.

Braking under various conditions.

Vision.

Residential driving.

Turns, stops starts at intersection.

Turning and backing under various conditions.

Sign the permit.

Drive on the streets.

Go over driving rules.

As needed by students, the
2nd
drive training session may include:

Overview of 1st session.

Residential parking.

Lane changes.

Turns and intersection right, left, and U-turn.

3-point turns.

Uphill/downhill parking.

Practice doing reverse.

As needed by students, the
3rd
drive training session may include:

Overview of 1st and 2nd sessions.

Freeway driving.

Entering and exiting freeways.

Night-time driving (depending upon appointment time).

DMV certification of completion behind the wheel training.

Practice test’s route if it’s possible.

As needed by students, the
4th
drive training session may include:

Overview of 1st, 2nd, and 3rd sessions.

Provide further guidance to help strengthen weak points and answer any questions.

Drive on regularly visited roads and in common areas.

Driving rules and best practices.

Overall “Drive Test” practice review.

Emergency situations and safety standards.

Car equipment breakdown and malfunction dangers.
