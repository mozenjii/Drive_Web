Consulting FAQ's
What Consulting services does PSC offer?
PSC offers various consulting services such as:
Safety Assessments
Ergonomic Assessments
Create Injury & Illness Prevention Programs
Create Emergency & Disaster Preparedness Plans
And much more!
Contact PSC so we can identify your Consulting needs.
Will PSC’s Consultant travel outside of San Diego Region?
PSC’s Consultants are very flexible and will travel nationally to assist you with your consulting needs.
Do I receive a Summary report after Consulting is completed?
Yes. Our Consultant will provide you with a Summary Report for your records.
Who do I contact regarding my Consulting needs?
Complete the Consulting Quote form and a PSC member will contact you shortly to discuss your needs in more detail. You can also contact us at 858-275-2738.
