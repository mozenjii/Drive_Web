Drivers Education For Teens
Requirements for the Driving Test
Be 15 ½ years old to take the written test and 16 years old to get your license
Completed drivers education
Complete behind-the-wheel training
Practice with a licensed adult 25 yrs or older for 6 months from the date of issue of your permit.

Getting a Driving Permit in California
California requires that any person under the age of 18 must attend driver’s education classes and receive a certificate of completion for the course from a DMV approved provider. You can either complete this requirement using an online classroom format or traditional classroom option.
Online Education
Complete your California Drivers Education Course online.

Everything is done ONLINE!
Take the coursework when it’s convenient with your schedule
Take our online Driver’s Education Course and Receive a DMV Recognized Certificate of Completion, necessary for obtaining your Learners Permit and for taking the Driver License Exam.
Behind the Wheel (BTW) Training
Behind the Wheel (BTW) is 6 hours of training with a licensed instructor. We schedule 3 appointments for 2 hours each. The instructor will pick you up at home, work, or school and teach you all the basics of operating a vehicle safely and legally. This fee is paid in advance to the Pacific Safety Center prior to your first appointment. At the end of the 6 hours, you will receive a Certificate of Completion in Drivers Training. You must finish all three classes within eight months, beginning the day of your first class.
All of our instructors are licensed through the DMV, professionally trained, bonded, and responsible.
