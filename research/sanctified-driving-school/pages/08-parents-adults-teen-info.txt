What Parents Can Expect

Progress Reports:
We provide feedback after each lesson so you know what your teen is learning and where they need improvement.

Road Test Readiness:
Our instructors ensure your teen is fully prepared for their driving test, covering everything from proper vehicle control, navigating intersections, and test day expectations.

Safety First Approach:
We emphasize defensive driving, accident prevention, and awareness of road laws.

How You Can Support Your Teen Driver

✔
Encourage Practice:
The more they practice, the more confident they will become. Supervised driving outside of lessons helps reinforce skills.
✔
Stay Positive:
Learning to drive can be stressful—patience and encouragement go a long way!
✔
Lead by Example:
Your teen learns from watching you drive. Model safe habits like using turn signals, avoiding distractions, and obeying speed limits.
✔
Ask Questions:
If you’re unsure how to support your teen, our instructors are happy to provide guidance.

Common Questions

DMV Requirements for Provisional Permit

To obtain a provisional permit you must be at least 15 1/2 years old.

Must complete a California State Approved Driver’s Education Course (Online or Classroom) - You can start either course at any age but it’s better to wait until you are 15 years old but must be at least 15 1/2 to take the written permit test. You will be issued a Certificate of Completion when you have successfully completed the course. An original Completion Certificate must be presented to the DMV when taking your test.

Fill out the DMV application DL 44 Application online at
dmv.ca.gov

Provide your social security number

Obtain your parents’ or guardians’ signatures

Present an acceptable birth date/legal presence document (Passport). This needs to be an original, copies will not be accepted.

Bring 2 Forms of Proof of California Residency.

Rental or Lease aggrement

Mortgage Bill

Home Utility Bill

Tax Return

Employment document

Pay the required application fee ($41.00 current price)

Pass an eye exam

Have your picture taken

Give a thumb print

Pass a written exam permit test - If you incorrectly answer 9 or more questions (46 total questions) on the exam, you must wait 7 days before retaking the written exam. You have 3 chances to pass the test.

What You Need to Get your Driver’s License

Hold your permit for a minimum of 6 months.

If you are between 15 1/2 and 18 years old you must also provide proof that you:

Completed 6 Hours of Driver’s Training Behind the Wheel. Sanctified Driving School will give you the Certificate for Free when you have completed our 6 Hour Behind the Wheel Course.

Have driven with a licensed driver for 50 hours of practice, 10 hours of which are night time driving, who will certify you have done these hours.

If you are at least 17 1/2, you may obtain a permit without taking a drivers education course. However to get a license, you must bring proof that you have completed drivers education and drivers training or wait until you are 18 years old.

Please note that the provisional instruction permit (driver’s permit) is not valid until you start your behind-the-wheel driver’s training with an instructor or reach the age of 17 1/2.

How many times can you fail a driving test in California ?

In California, you have three attempts to pass the driving test within 12 months of paying the initial application fee. If you fail the road test, you must wait 14 days (not including the day of the failure) to retake it and pay a retest fee for each subsequent attempt.

How many mistakes can you make during CA DMV drive test?

To pass the road test portion you must make no more than 30 errors and no critical driving errors, which will result in an automatic failure.

Is parallel parking required in California driving tests?

While parallel parking isn’t explicitly required in California DMV driving test, you will be expected to demonstrate your ability to pull up to a curb and reverse in a straight line for a few car lengths.
