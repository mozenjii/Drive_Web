FAQs (Frequent Asked Questions)
1. Does the service include pick up and drop off?
The service includes pick-up and drop-off as long as the student is located 12 miles away from our headquarters (5663 Lincoln Ave., Suite A, Cypress, CA 90630). Students who live further than 12 miles can come to our location or meet us halfway.

2. How Can I see the available openings?
You can see the available openings when you log in to your account, or you can always call the office to find the available openings during office hours (Mon – Friday from 8:00 am – 5:00 pm, and Saturday from 9:00 am-1:00 pm).

3. Can I choose my instructor?
Yes, you can choose your instructor when you schedule your driving lessons.

4. What are the hours for behind-the-wheel lessons?
For behind-the-wheel lessons, we work every day of the week. The hours are Monday – Friday from 7:00 – 10:00 pm and Saturday- Sunday from 7:00 am – 8:00 pm.

5. Are the driving lessons for minors and adults?
Both adults and minors can register for any program of their preference.

6. How Do I schedule my appts after I register?
After you register and pay, the system will send you a username and a password to your email. When you get the email, go back to the website and click on login: Driving lessons log in, where you enter your login information and log in to your student’s account.

7. Should I schedule my lesson in advance?
Yes, we always recommend booking your lessons 2 or 3 weeks in advance if you want to get the days and the times that are more convenient for you. Especially on weekends and busy seasons like Summer Break, Spring Break, or Winter Break

8. Would I get charged if I canceled my lessons the same day?
Yes, for same-day cancellations, no-shows, or same-day reschedules, there is a $75 charge.

10. How long do I have to complete my program?
If you are registered to a behind-the-wheel program, you will have one year from the registration date to complete it. If you are registered to a Driver’s Ed program + Behind the Wheel program, you have one year and a half from the registration date.

11. Do I get a refund if I decide not to continue with the program?
There are no refunds for online services.

There are no refunds for Behind the Wheel services that have been rendered, but you can request a refund for services that have not been rendered adjusted to the current price by paying a $25 refund fee.

12. What does it mean within 12 miles from Kanor and more than 12 miles from Kanor?
The distinction between “within 12 miles from Kanor” and “more than 12 miles from Kanor” refers to the distance from the location of Kanor. You can check the distance from your pick-up location to Kanor’s office (5661 Lincoln Ave. Suite A Cypress CA 90630)

Within 12 miles from Kanor
: This means any location that is up to 12 miles away from Kanor.

More than 12 miles from Kanor
: This means any location that is further than 12 miles away from Kanor.

If you need more details or have specific questions about these distances, please let us know!

13. What should I bring on my lessons?
For your driving lessons, please make sure to bring the following:

Your valid learner’s permit or driver’s license

Your glasses or contact lenses
, if you need them to drive

Comfortable shoes
(avoid flip-flops/sandals or heels)

Any required paperwork
we may have asked you to sign or bring

A positive attitude and readiness to learn!

14. What happens if I forget to bring my Learner's Permit or DL?
If you arrive without the required documents (such as your permit or license) we may not be able to conduct your lesson for safety and legal reasons. In such cases, the lesson may be canceled and it is subject to a same-day cancellation fee of $75. Please double-check before your appointment to ensure everything is in order

15. What happens if I wear sandals or heels to my driving lessons?
If you arrive wearing inappropriate footwear like sandals, we may not be able to conduct your lesson for safety and legal reasons. In such cases, the lesson may be canceled and it is subject to a same-day cancellation fee of $75.
