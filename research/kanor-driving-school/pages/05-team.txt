Meet the Kanor Team

At Kanor Driving School, our instructors are more than just licensed—they’re experienced, patient, and committed to helping you succeed. Each one is certified by the California DMV and has completed professional training in safety, traffic laws, and teaching techniques.

We take pride in hiring instructors with clean driving records, strong communication skills, and a real passion for helping students feel confident behind the wheel.

All of our instructors are employees—not independent contractors—so they’re carefully selected, fully supervised, and part of a team that works together to provide the best learning experience possible.

With Kanor, you’re learning from professionals who care.
