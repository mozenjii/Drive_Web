Accessibility Commitment for Kanor Driving School, Inc.

At
Kanor Driving School, Inc.
, we are committed to making our digital presence as accessible and inclusive as reasonably possible for all users, including individuals with disabilities. Our goal is to improve the usability of
https://www.kanor.net
and to support a more accessible experience for everyone, regardless of their abilities or the technologies they use.

Our Approach to Accessibility

We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), which define internationally recognized standards for digital accessibility. While full compliance cannot always be guaranteed, we strive to implement improvements where feasible and regularly review accessibility-related aspects of our website. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we are committed to improving the experience over time as technologies, standards, and user needs evolve.

may utilize tools such as the OneTap accessibility toolbar. This interface provides users with a range of helpful features, including:

The availability and effectiveness of these features depend on the website's configuration and ongoing maintenance.

While we strive to ensure accessibility, we cannot guarantee that every part of

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback. If you experience any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us: Email:
support@kanor.net
