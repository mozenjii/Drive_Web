Traffic School Q & A

How do I know if I have to take Traffic School?
You must have received a minor moving violation, and you must have received a notice from the DMV containing a list of licensed courses you can enroll in. If you have yet to receive the notice from the Court or are uncertain about your eligibility, you can contact the Court directly to inquire and clarify your status.

If I took traffic school for a ticket, can I retake traffic school for another ticket?
You should not have attended traffic school within the last 18 months to be able to take traffic school again.

Can I take traffic school if I have a commercial driver's license?
You cannot take traffic school if you possess a commercial driver’s license and receive a ticket while operating a commercial vehicle. However, if you received your ticket while operating your personal vehicle, you may be eligible to take Traffic School; you can contact the Court directly to inquire and clarify your status.

Can I take traffic School if I was driving under the influence of drugs or alcohol?
Your ticket is not eligible for traffic school if your ticket was given due to alcohol or drug-related offenses.

What is the process after finding out I'm eligible for traffic school?
You should contact the Court if you want to attend traffic school; the clerk or judge will provide you with a deadline to complete the traffic school program. You’ll need to pay for your ticket and the traffic school fees to the Court. Afterward, sign up and pay for a traffic school program provided by an approved traffic school.

What is the difference between the Online Traffic School Program and the Home Study Book Program?
The difference lies in the delivery method of the traffic school program. The online program makes all the study materials and final test accessible through your online account. Upon completing the program and passing the final test, your traffic school completion will be automatically submitted to the Court. On the other hand, with the home study book option, you’ll order the book online, and a physical copy of the materials and final test will be mailed to you. After finishing the program and sending back the completed final test for grading, the office will submit the traffic school completion to the Court.

What is the online Traffic school material content?
The online material consists of eleven chapters that you can complete at your own pace. At the end of each chapter, there are short quizzes to check your understanding. Your progress is securely saved in your account, allowing you to log in and out at any time. The program typically takes 6–8 hours to finish and is timed, as required by the DMV. Once all chapters are completed, you will take a final exam with 25 questions. To pass, you must earn a minimum score of 70% (18 correct answers). Upon passing, the system will automatically submit your traffic school completion to the court.

If you do not pass on your first attempt, you will be given one additional opportunity to retake the final test.

What is the home-study Traffic School material content?
The home-study Booklet can be ordered online or picked up at the office. The Traffic School booklet has eleven chapters that can be read at your own pace.

What should I do when I receive my home-study Traffic School Booklet?
Upon receiving your booklet, you must thoroughly read and study its contents. When you feel prepared, fill out your name and Driver’s license information as requested on each final test page. Proceed to take the final test, which comprises 25 questions. Sign and date the last page before sending it back to our office for grading. Upon passing the final test with a minimum grade of 70% (18 correct answers), we will submit your traffic school completion to the Court. In case you don’t pass the final test on the first attempt, you’ll have an opportunity for a second attempt. We will mail you a second final test to your house.

How long do I have to complete my traffic school program?
After paying the fees for your ticket and traffic school to the Court, they will give a due date or deadline to finish traffic school.

What happens after I complete Traffic school?
If you have a noncommercial driver’s license, completing your traffic school program will hide your point from your public driving record. However, these rules will differ if you have a different type of Driver’s license (commercial). Completing traffic school will also prevent your insurance premium from increasing due to the ticket.

It’s a good idea to follow up with the court or DMV to ensure they received your certificate and processed your completion. Some courts provide online systems to check the status of your ticket or record.

Can I get a refund for my online Traffic School Program?
We do not issue refunds for the online Traffic School Program.
