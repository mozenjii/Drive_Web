Driver Education

Our online Driver’s Education Course will fulfill your mandatory California DMV Driver’s Education Requirements.
This course is DMV licensed.
You can take it any time and anywhere at your convenience.

The course is divided into 11 units. There is a quiz at the end of each unit. When you have completed all the units, there will be a 30-question final exam. Once you have passed the final exam, a DMV Certificate of Completion for Drivers Education will be mailed to you. This certificate is valid at every DMV office in the state of California.

We accept all credit cards and PayPal for your payment options.

The total price for the course is
$35.00

Please note this is not a traffic school course, but a drivers ed course for teenagers.
