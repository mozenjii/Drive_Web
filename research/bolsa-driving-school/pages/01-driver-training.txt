Driver Training

Bolsa Driving School Driver Training program consists of 6 hours of professional behind-the-wheel instruction. For your convenience this course is divided into three lessons, two hours each. Students will learn basic driving skills to operate a motor vehicle safely and proper driving techniques that will prepare them for the DMV drive test. We offer free home, school or work pick-up and return (within the same vicinity). Lessons are offered Monday through Sunday (morning, afternoon and evening).

After completed 6 hours driving course, students will receive a certificate of completion as required by the DMV. Students will also receive an evaluation from driving instructor which will indicate the specific driving tasks may need improvement.

We provide professional driving school for teens, adults. All instructors are licensed by DMV. English fluently, young, on time, cars installed brake/gas pedals for both student and instructor, vehicle safety checked frequently, insurance covered.
