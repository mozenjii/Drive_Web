NO refunds will be issued. Unused, prepaid, instruction can be transferred to a sibling within a one-year time frame.

Unused, prepaid, instruction does not expire. However, if not completed with-in a one-year time frame, student may be subject to prorated current rates.

24-hour notice of cancellation required. Less than 24-hour notice will be subject to $50.00 cancellation fee.

If no notice of cancellation is provided, and the instructor arrives for the scheduled appointment, the full price of the lesson will be required. This also applies if the student is unprepared for lesson, IE: does not have permit, does not have required vision aids, arrives to meeting location more than 15 minutes late, etc.

Students paying for lessons individually, non- bundled payments, are subject to rate increases.

Turlock Driving School reserves the right to refuse service to anyone deemed unsafe to operate a vehicle.
