How It Works
​

✓

Step 1: Book your first lesson online

Use our website to schedule a lesson at your

convenience. Choose “Bakersfield” and make

sure you already have your California DMV

instruction permit.

✓

Step 2: We pick you up

Your instructor will meet you at home, school,

or another approved location within all Bakersfield ZIP codes. No need to arrange

transportation.

✓

Step 3: The lesson begins

We start with calm, clear instruction and

practice routes tailored for beginners — no freeways, no pressure. Everything is explained

before you try it. Freeways are on the 2nd lesson.

​
​
​

✓

Step 4: We teach, then you drive

Our DMV-licensed instructors demonstrate

first, then guide you step-by-step. We

prioritize safety and confidence over rushing.

​
​
​

✓

Step 5: Build over time

We recommend at least 3 two-hour lessons

(6 hours total) to meet DMV requirements

and fully prepare for the test. You can book a

your own pace. Many students take more than

the minimum of 3 lessons. We are here to help.

✓

Step 6: Have us take you to the DMV to test

Once you're ready, we offer an optional

DMV Test Lesson that includes our car,

instructor, and 1-on-1 prep before your actual

exam.

Frequently Asked

Questions
​

✓

Do you pick up from high schools

in Bakersfield?

Yes. We offer home or school pickup anywhere

in Bakersfield — including Highland, East, Foothill, Independence, Stockdale, and Heritage Christian. You’ll choose your pickup location when booking.

✓

How soon can I get on the schedule?

We currently have immediate openings in Bakersfield. Most students can get their first

lesson within a day.

✓

Can my parent ride along during the lesson?

Yes. We welcome parent ride-alongs. Some

parents like to observe the first lesson to understand what’s being taught and how

they can reinforce it at home.

​
​
​

✓

Do I need a permit before I start lessons?

Yes. California law requires a learner’s permit

for behind-the-wheel instruction. You can

begin your lessons with us as soon as you

pass your written test at the DMV. You must

bring your permit to all of your lessons.

​
​
​

✓

Can I use your car for the DMV test?

Yes. Once your training is complete, we offer a

special DMV Test Lesson. We’ll review

everything, drive you to the test, and you’ll

use our car for the exam.

Please call
(661) 822-1990
to set up the

special DMV Test Lesson. We must set up the appointment at the DMV.

✓

How many lessons are required?

California requires 6 hours of professional

behind-the-wheel instruction. We divide that

into three 2-hour lessons — structured,

strategic, and always instructor-led. Many

students choose to do more for extra prep, and

we’ll work with you until you’re fully ready.
