The "Quiet Start" Protocol: Demonstrate, Guide, Then Drive

​

“Anxiety in the cockpit comes from two things: the unknown, and the pressure of traffic,” says Ferry. “We diffuse that immediately. We don't just throw a student into the deep end of traffic to 'toughen them up.' We start in quiet, low-stress residential neighborhoods where they can focus on vehicle dynamics without the fear of other motorists.”

The curriculum follows a strict
"Explain, Demonstrate, Attempt"
loop:

​

Explanation:
The instructor explains the physics and logic of the maneuver (the "Why").

Demonstration:
The instructor performs the maneuver first, allowing the student to observe timing and control without pressure.

The "Quiet Start":
The student attempts the skill in a controlled environment. Only after mastering the mechanics does the lesson move to complex traffic.
