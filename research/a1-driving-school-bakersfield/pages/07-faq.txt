How old do I need to be to start driver ed?
At age 15, you can begin driver education. When you are 15 1/2 years old, you can apply for an instruction permit. The permit allows you to drive only with supervision from a parent, guardian, or driver over 25 years old with an unrestricted California license. After six months and 50 hours of supervised driving practice, you can take the road test and get a provisional license.
What safety equipment are your cars equipped with?
All vehicles are equipped& with a passenger-side brake and rearview mirror for the driving instructor, we also have cameras that record the entire lesson.
