Driving Lessons in Lake Isabella with A1 Driving School
A1 Driving School is now offering behind-the-wheel driving lessons in Lake Isabella — bringing the same trusted instruction that’s earned us over 300 five-star reviews across Kern County.

We’re honored to serve this community not just as newcomers, but as the school personally recommended by the longtime owners of Coach’s Driving School, who faithfully taught Lake Isabella students for decades before retiring.

Whether you're a teen preparing for your first license or a parent looking for a safe, reputable program, our DMV-licensed instructors provide clear, calm, and structured one-on-one instruction — with home, school, and local pickup available.

Over 25 years. Tens of thousands trained. A legacy we’re proud to continue — now in Lake Isabella.
