Learn to drive with our comprehensive California state-licensed driver education and driver training programs.
If you are under the age of 18:

✓
Step 1.
Take the 30-hour online class.

✓
Step 2.
Go to the DMV to complete the knowledge test and receive your instruction permit. You will need to bring a printed copy of your Certificate of Completion from the online course.

✓
Step 3.
Register below for behind-the-wheel lessons and schedule online.

✓
Step 4.
Practice driving with a parents/guardian for 50 hours (10 at night).

✓
Step 5.
After... your 16th birthday, you've completed all 6 hours of driving lessons, and you've had your instructional permit for six months, you can take the road test at the DMV.

​

If you are over the age of 18:

​

✓
Step 1:
Go to the DMV to complete the knowledge test and receive your instruction permit.

✓
Step 2:
Register below for behind-the-wheel lessons and schedule online.

✓
Step 3:
Take the DMV driving test.

​

Every lesson combines professional instruction, instructor demonstration, and personalized behind-the-wheel coaching to help students become safe, confident drivers.
