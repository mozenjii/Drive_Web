What You Will Learn
This course is intended for drivers of any age who need to attend traffic school. Within 30 minutes of completion, you will receive an official certificate and your results will be automatically forwarded to the California courts. This will allow you to continue the process of restoring your driving privilege or making amendments to your driving record.
