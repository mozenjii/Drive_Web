What You Will Learn
Required for all drivers new to Illinois ages 18 to 20 who have never held a license and have never taken driver’s education in another state.
Syllabus:
Chapters 1 & 2: Complete Your Registration and New Driver Introduction
Chapters 3 & 4: Driver Attitude and Basic Control Tasks
Chapters 5 & 6: Defensive Driving and Sharing the Road
Chapters 7 & 8: Signs, Signals, and Road Markings and Right-of-Way
Chapters 9 & 10: Speeding & Passing
Chapters 11 & 12: Stopping Distance and Driving Environments
Chapters 13 & 14: Environmental Hazards and Distracted Driving
Chapters 15 & 16: Aggressive Driving and Driver Impairment
Chapters 17 & 18: Driving Under the Influence and Medications and Driving
Chapters 19 & 20: Emergencies and Safety Features
Chapters 21 & 22: Key Vehicle Maintenance and Getting On The Road
Chapter 23: Illinois Adult SPIDER Driver's Ed Final Quiz
Chapter 24: Course Completion Information
Chapter 25: You Did It! Now Tell Us What You Think
Can I take the course on my phone?
Instead of spending the 6 hours it takes to complete the driver's ed course in a classroom, you can take it online. Better yet, with Coastline Academy you can do the entire course on your phone. Or jump back and forth between your phone, tablet, and computer. You'll get the information you need to pass your permit test and start driving.
