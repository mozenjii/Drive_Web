Learn with Confidence | EXPRESS DRIVING SCHOOL

6 Hours Driving Package

Experience personalized driver’s training with our 6-hour private in-car lessons led by a licensed DMV driving instructor. Spread across three days, each session is dedicated to enhancing your behind-the-wheel skills. Our fleet of vehicles is fully insured, meticulously maintained, and consistently reliable. Equipped with dual controls and subjected to annual DMV safety inspections, our cars prioritize your safety. Your journey to confident and skilled driving begins with us.

Individual Driving Lessons

Discover flexibility with our single lessons, each spanning a 2-hour session to accommodate your specific needs. Whether you’re a just new driver, a foreign student, or a newcomer to the U.S. aiming to learn and obtain your California driver’s license, this lesson is designed just for you. We also offer customization for experienced drivers looking to refine and polish their driving skills. Your journey to improved driving competence starts here.
