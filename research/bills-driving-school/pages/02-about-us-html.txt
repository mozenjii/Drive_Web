About Bill's Driving School

Bills Driving School was established in 2014. We are a bonded and insured driving school that offers all driving services for both teens and adults. We have multiple instructors and services many areas throughout Sacramento and San Joaquin counties. Our instruction provides behind-the-wheel training and DMV service to people of all ages.

Why Bill's Driving School?

At Bills Driving School we want to ensure that all students learn how to drive safely, efficiently, and in accordance with DMV regulations. It is our mission to do everything we can to ensure students pass driving exams on their first try, and this is shown with our 95% first time pass rate. Our learning curriculum provides students with practice all the way up to their drive test with the DMV, and we offer options to space lessons out to keep students continuously practice with qualified instructors across many locations.
