60-Hour of Driving School Instructor Training Program

Become a Certified Driving Instructor.

Driving School For You is proud to announce our new 60-Hour Driving Instructor Training Program. This comprehensive, DMV-approved course prepares you to become a licensed driving instructor in California, combining classroom instruction with hands-on training from experienced professionals.

Led by Dr. Brian Polte, an award-winning driving instructor with years of experience teaching both students and future instructors, this program gives you the skills, knowledge, and confidence to start your own rewarding career in driver education.

Call

DMV-Approved Certification Program

Complete our 60-hour training program and receive the certification needed to apply for your California driving instructor license. Join a growing industry with flexible hours and the satisfaction of helping new drivers stay safe on the road.

20 Hours

Behind-the-wheel instruction

Hands-on training in a dual-controlled vehicle, focusing on lesson planning, hazard perception, student evaluation, and safe intervention strategies.

40 Hours

Classroom instruction

Deep dive into traffic laws, defensive driving techniques, risk management, and instructional psychology. Learn to effectively communicate complex concepts.

Program Requirements

To enroll in our 60-Hour Driving School Instructor Training Program, you must meet the following California DMV requirements:

Age Requirement

Must be at least 21 years old

Valid License

Hold a valid California driver's license for at least 3 years.

Clean Record

Have a clean Motor Vehicle Record (MVR).

High School Education

High school diploma or equivalent required

Background Check

Pass a background check

Physical Exam

Complete DMV-required medical examination
