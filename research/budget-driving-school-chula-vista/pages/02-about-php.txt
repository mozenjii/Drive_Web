About

Budget Driving School LLC offers Driver Education (the 4 day classroom course), Online Driver Education, Driver Training (Behind-The-Wheel) instruction for tickets. Our school is committed to offering the best possible training at the most affordable rate.

All Budget Driving School LLC instructors follow the state guidelines and have the highest standards. Both Driver Education and Driver Training are required by the State of California for any person under the age of 18 to become a licensed driver. All instructors are licensed by Department of Motor Vehicles.
