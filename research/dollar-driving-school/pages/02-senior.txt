NEW CALIFORNIA RESIDENT:

Many newcomers to the state find that they do not have the skills to easily cope with California’s intricate traffic problems. Freeway driving requires special skills. Thorough knowledge of the California motor vehicle code is imperative. Therefore, all new residents are urged to avail themselves of professional instructors.

SENIOR CITIZEN:

When a person grows older, unless their vision and their slower reactions are improved, they may no longer be a safe driver. Instructors with experience in the needs of senior citizens are a specialty at Dollar Driving School of Ventura. We can also help to get your license back by improving your driving skills in the event the Department of Motor Vehicles takes away your driving privilege, where and when improvement is feasible.
