About Us
Qualified Instructors
We are ready to serve you we have professional staff and qualified instructors licensed with the state of California as a driving instructors. All of our cars are equipped with dual gas and brake system on instructor's side. We provide driving trainings for teenage, adults and seniors. Our headquarter is located in Modesto California and we serve all major cities
near by.
Requirements
Inorder to register for behind the wheel trainings you must have your driving permit and be eligible to drive. Teenagers must complete the first class of BTW with an instructor and get their permit signed off after the first class inorder to train with their parents and after completing the entire 6 hours course they will be eligible for their certificate.
Class Info
We are open 7 days a week except major holidays our class and session hours are from 8 am to 7 pm you can reach us by phone, email or text to help with registrations or questions and you can also visit us here on our webpage to get more info and register for courses as well as to make a payment.
