FAQ

Before you book

How many hours does California require?
Under 18, the DMV requires 6 hours of professional behind-the-wheel training with a licensed instructor, plus 50 hours of supervised practice with a parent or guardian — 10 of those at night. DMV Complete covers all 6 professional hours.
Can I use your car for the road test?
Yes. Our cars are registered, insured and DMV-compliant for road-test use. A warm-up drive and the ride to and from the test centre are included.
Do you pick up from school or work?
Anywhere in our service area, at no extra charge. Set the address when you book.
I failed the test before. Can you help?
That's a large part of what we do. We diagnose exactly what the examiner marked you down on, drill it, then run the real route until it's automatic.
What's the cancellation policy?
Reschedule free up to 24 hours before, straight from your confirmation email. Inside 24 hours a partial fee applies.
Do you teach adults and licence transfers?
Yes — roughly a third of our students are over 18, including drivers transferring a licence from another country.
