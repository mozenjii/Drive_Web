Common questions

Before you book

How many hours does California actually require?
For drivers under 18, the DMV requires 6 hours of professional behind-the-wheel training with a licensed instructor, plus 50 hours of supervised practice with a parent or guardian, 10 of which must be at night. Our DMV Complete package covers all 6 professional hours.
Can I use your car for my DMV road test?
Yes. Our vehicles are registered, insured and DMV-compliant for road-test use. We include a warm-up drive beforehand and take you to and from the test centre.
Do you pick up from school or work?
Yes, anywhere in our service area at no extra charge — home, high school, or your workplace. Just set the address when you book.
What if my teen is extremely nervous?
Tell us when booking and we will start in an empty lot rather than traffic. A number of our instructors specialise in anxious first-time drivers, and we never put a student on a freeway before they are ready.
What is your cancellation policy?
Reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the lesson, directly from your confirmation email. Inside 24 hours, a partial fee applies.
Do you teach adults and licence transfers?
Yes. Roughly a third of our students are over 18 — new drivers, people returning after years away, and drivers transferring a licence from another country.
