Questions

Before you book.

How many hours does California actually require?
For drivers under 18, the DMV requires six hours of professional behind-the-wheel training with a licensed instructor, alongside 50 hours of supervised practice with a parent or guardian, ten of which must be at night. Our DMV Complete package covers all six professional hours.
Will I have the same instructor throughout?
Yes. That is the point of the school. Unless you ask to change, the instructor who takes your first lesson takes every subsequent one, including test day.
Can I use your car for the DMV road test?
Yes. Our vehicles are registered, insured and DMV-compliant for road-test use, and we include the warm-up drive and transport to and from the test centre.
Do you collect from school or work?
Anywhere within our service area at no additional charge. Simply set the address when booking.
What if the student is very anxious?
Tell us when you book. We begin in a quiet lot rather than in traffic, and we do not put any student on a freeway before they are ready. Several of our instructors work primarily with nervous drivers.
What is the cancellation policy?
Reschedule without charge up to 24 hours before the lesson, directly from your confirmation email. Within 24 hours, a partial fee applies.
