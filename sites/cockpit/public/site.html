Questions

Before you book

How many hours does California require?
Under 18, the DMV requires 6 hours of professional behind-the-wheel training with a licensed instructor, plus 50 hours of supervised practice with a parent or guardian — 10 of those at night. The Teen Driver Program covers all 6 professional hours.
Can I use your car for the DMV road test?
Yes. Our cars are registered, insured and DMV-compliant for road-test use. The Road-Test Day package includes a warm-up drive and the ride to and from the test centre.
Do you pick up from school or work?
Anywhere inside our service area at no extra charge. Set the address when you book.
I already failed once. Can you help?
That is a large part of what we do. We work out exactly what the examiner marked, drill it, then run the real route until it stops feeling like a test.
What is the cancellation policy?
Reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before, straight from your confirmation email. Inside 24 hours a partial fee applies.
